# Co-Dreaming — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** Shared dream journals for couples and close friends — the app surfaces the
eerie overlaps between your dreams, adding a social layer to the most private experience
humans have.

**Category / niche:** Social / dreams / relationships.

## Insight (the wedge)
Solo dream journaling has no network effect — that loneliness is why dream apps die. Make
dreams *shareable* between two trusted people and you get the retention loop every solo app
lacks: you come back to see what *they* dreamed, and to find the uncanny connections.

## Target user
Couples and pairs of close friends who are into dreams, astrology-adjacent curiosity, or
just each other. Relationship/intimacy angle first; hobbyist second.

## Signature mechanic
**Overlap detection**: both partners log dreams; the app highlights shared symbols, themes,
emotions, or timing ("you both dreamed about water this week") and turns them into little
moments of intimacy and conversation.

## MVP scope
1. Private shared space for two (invite a partner).
2. Fast dream capture (voice or text, morning-friendly).
3. Overlap/theme detection across both journals.
4. A weekly "dream digest" of your shared patterns.
5. Gentle prompts that spark conversation about the overlaps.

## Why it stands out
It converts a solo, churny category into a *relationship ritual* with a built-in reason to
return — and a viral loop (you invite the person you dream about).

## Monetization
Freemium; paid unlocks deeper overlap analysis / longer history; couples framing supports
higher willingness to pay.

## Top risk → mitigation
Two-sided activation (needs both partners) → design the single-player experience to be
valuable alone, with overlap as the upgrade that pulls the partner in.

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design a "co-dreaming" app for couples and close friends: a private shared dream journal
> that detects and surfaces eerie overlaps between two people's dreams (shared symbols,
> themes, timing) and turns them into intimacy and conversation. Spec the 5 MVP screens with
> copy, the overlap-detection approach, the invite/viral loop, and how single-player stays
> valuable before the partner joins. Voice: intimate, a little uncanny, playful — not clinical.
