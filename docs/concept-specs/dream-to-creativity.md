# Dream-to-Creativity Capture — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A tool for artists, writers, and founders to *plant a problem* before sleep
and *harvest the solution* at 4am before it evaporates — selling capture, not dreaming.

**Category / niche:** Creativity / productivity / sleep-adjacent.

## Insight (the wedge)
The "I solved it in a dream" myth is real (the sewing machine needle, the benzene ring) — but
everyone forgets the dream by breakfast. You're not selling the dream; you're selling
**capture**. That reframing dodges the lucid-dreaming churn problem entirely and points at a
paying, motivated professional audience.

## Target user
Creative professionals and founders who obsess over problems: writers, designers, engineers,
researchers. People who already believe in incubation and "sleeping on it."

## Signature mechanic
**Plant → capture → harvest**: at night, articulate the specific problem (a "seed"); in the
dark, half-asleep, voice-capture whatever surfaces (zero-friction, eyes-closed); by morning,
an auto-transcribed, cleaned-up **digest** that ties fragments back to the seed.

## MVP scope
1. Problem "seed" entry + a pre-sleep priming ritual.
2. Eyes-closed voice capture (huge tap target, dark, silent, no typing).
3. Auto-transcription + overnight fragment collection.
4. Morning digest linking captures to the seed, with a "keep/discard" pass.
5. Seed history + "solutions harvested" archive.

## Why it stands out
No competitor frames dreaming as a *capture/productivity* problem for professionals. It's
defensible, B2B-adjacent (teams, studios), and sidesteps the toy-novelty trap.

## Monetization
Prosumer subscription; team/studio tier; potential integration with note tools.

## Top risk → mitigation
"Does it actually work?" skepticism → lean on incubation research + testimonials; the value
is real capture of hypnagogic ideas regardless of "dream magic."

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design a creativity tool for writers/designers/founders that turns dreams into usable ideas.
> Flow: plant a problem "seed" before sleep → eyes-closed, dark, one-tap voice capture during
> the night → an auto-transcribed morning digest linking fragments back to the seed. Spec the
> 5 MVP screens with copy, the friction-free nighttime capture UX (designed for a
> half-asleep person in the dark), the morning digest format, and the positioning that sells
> "capture," not "dreaming." Voice: sharp, creative, a little mystical but grounded.
