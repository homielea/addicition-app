# Menopause-as-Weapon — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A menopause app that stops tracking symptoms and starts arming women —
turning dismissed complaints into evidence, scripts, and leverage against the doctors,
bosses, and partners who don't believe them.

**Category / niche:** Femtech / menopause & perimenopause.

## Insight (the wedge)
The #1 unspoken menopause wound isn't physiological — it's *epistemic*: being told your
reality isn't real. Every femtech app is a symptom diary that produces a chart and a feeling
of being "managed." Nobody sells women **leverage**. Convert *gaslit* into *documented*.

## Target user
Women 40s–50s whose symptoms are dismissed by doctors ("you're fine"), misread by partners,
or penalized at work. Motivated, underserved, and furious about not being believed.

## Signature mechanic
The **credibility engine**: one tap logs a symptom with timestamp + cycle context; the app
then auto-generates the artifacts that create leverage —
- a one-page clinical summary to slap on a dismissive doctor's desk,
- a scripted, evidence-backed conversation for the boss or partner,
- a receipts view that answers "you're overreacting" with data.

Plus a **partner companion app** (bundled) that quietly coaches the husband/partner:
"she's not mad at you, here's what's happening, here's what NOT to say this week."

## MVP scope
1. Fast symptom + context logging (one-tap).
2. Auto-generated doctor one-pager (PDF/share).
3. Conversation scripts (doctor / partner / employer).
4. Receipts timeline.
5. (Fast-follow) partner companion app.

## Why it stands out
Two unserved customers in one household (her + the partner), and a *weaponized* data model
instead of a passive tracker. Whoever arms women instead of visualizing their decline owns
the category.

## Monetization
Consumer subscription; potential B2B via employers (menopause is a top workplace-attrition
issue) and via clinics who want the pre-visit summary.

## Top risk → mitigation
Medical-claim/liability surface → the app organizes *her own* reported data and prepares her
to talk to clinicians; it never diagnoses or advises treatment.
