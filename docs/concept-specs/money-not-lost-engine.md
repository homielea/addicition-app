# Money-Not-Lost Engine — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A sobriety/quit app that ditches the sterile day-counter and instead wires to
the actual dollars you *didn't* spend — then visibly spends them on you ("you just earned
the flight to Lisbon"). Recovery you can see in your bank account.

**Category / niche:** Addiction / behavior change (alcohol, vaping, gambling, any costly habit).

## Insight (the wedge)
The day-counter is abstract and brittle: a relapse crushes you back to zero. Money is concrete
and *additive* — a slip doesn't delete the trip you already funded. Reframe recovery around a
number that grows and *buys you things you want*, not a streak that shatters.

## Target user
People quitting an expensive habit (drinking, vaping, gambling, daily takeout/impulse spends)
who are motivated by tangible reward and demoralized by streak resets.

## Signature mechanic
**Earn-and-spend**: set a baseline spend; every day/period not spent accrues real dollar value;
the app lets you *allocate* the saved money toward a concrete goal and celebrates when you hit
it. A slip pauses accrual — it never wipes what you already banked.

## MVP scope
1. Baseline spend setup (per habit).
2. Money-saved accrual (additive, never reset).
3. Goal allocation ("this is buying the ___").
4. Milestone celebrations tied to real purchases.
5. Slip handling that pauses, never zeroes.

## Why it stands out
Fixes the #1 emotional flaw in quit-apps (the demoralizing reset) with a reward system that's
concrete, motivating, and screenshot-worthy.

## Monetization
Subscription; the ROI story sells itself ("you saved $400 this month, the app is $10").

## Top risk → mitigation
Feels gimmicky if shallow → tie savings to *emotionally real* goals and make the accrual/goal
loop central, not a side stat.

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design a quit/sobriety app whose core metric is money *not* spent on the habit — additive,
> never reset by a slip — that you allocate toward real goals and celebrate buying ("you just
> earned the flight to Lisbon"). Spec the 5 MVP screens with copy, the baseline→accrual→goal→
> milestone loop, how slips pause (not zero) the number, and the ROI-driven positioning. Voice:
> motivating, concrete, a little celebratory — never preachy.
