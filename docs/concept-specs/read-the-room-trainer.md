# Read-the-Room Trainer — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A gym for social cues, built honestly for neurodivergent adults — short clips,
"what's this person actually feeling / what do they want?", instant feedback. Not a cure, a
practice ground.

**Category / niche:** Emotional intelligence / neurodivergent-first.

## Insight (the wedge)
A huge, loyal, underserved population is told to "just read social cues" with zero tools to
actually practice. Specificity beats the vague "boost your EQ" mush — build the *gym*, not
another concept course, and build it *for* neurodivergent adults instead of at them.

## Target user
Neurodivergent adults (autistic, ADHD) who want to get better at reading social/emotional
cues on their own terms — plus anyone who finds subtext hard.

## Signature mechanic
**Cue reps**: short realistic clips or scenarios → "what is this person feeling? what do they
want?" → multiple-choice or open answer → instant, concrete feedback explaining the tells.
Difficulty scales; you build a measurable "read" skill like reps at a gym.

## MVP scope
1. Clip/scenario library tagged by cue type (sarcasm, discomfort, hidden ask, etc.).
2. The core rep: watch → answer → feedback that names the specific tells.
3. Difficulty progression + skill tracking.
4. "Why" explanations that teach the underlying signal, respectfully.
5. Streak-free progress (no shame; framed as training).

## Why it stands out
Neurodivergent-first framing + genuine practice reps is a category almost nobody serves well;
the audience is loyal and vocal, and the "gym not cure" honesty builds trust.

## Monetization
Subscription; potential partnerships with neurodivergent orgs / employers (workplace comms).

## Top risk → mitigation
Condescension / "fixing" framing → co-design with neurodivergent users, respectful "gym"
language, celebrate the skill without pathologizing the person.
