# Verified Niche Ranking — Strategy Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** The researched whitespace map for mental-health apps — where demand is loud
and competition is thin — used as raw material to generate or pressure-test new app concepts.

**Not an app.** This is a strategy brief: feed it to Fable 5 to generate concepts *inside*
the best niches, or to critique an idea against the real competitive picture.

## Method + caveats
Findings came from a fan-out research pass (multiple agents, cited sources). **Caveats:**
exact keyword volumes were partly blocked in-sandbox (some figures are from search snippets,
not primary fetches); market-size figures come from different firms with incompatible scope;
"zero app exists" claims rest on search results, not exhaustive App Store audits. Re-verify
before betting capital. Lens = *solo/indie founder* (low CAC, consumer-pays, no clinical/
insurance/employer dependency).

## The ranking (best demand-to-competition for a solo)

### 🥇 Men's emotional health, non-therapy framing
- Competition thin (Mettle ~only pure-play). Demand huge & creator-reachable (Huberman 300M+
  downloads; r/AskMen 7.3M). Only ~17% of men in treatment; suicide ~4× women. Verified
  hard signal: **"porn addiction help" ~30,749/mo (US)**, the #1 addiction help-search.
- → This became **Trojan Horse** (see status-quo spec).

### 🥈 Father/partner postpartum + estrangement grief
- Near-zero direct competitors. Paternal PPD ~8–10%, spiking **~25.6% at 3–6 months** — and
  **no father-facing app exists**. Estrangement: r/EstrangedAdultKids **+47% YoY**, ~27% of
  adults estranged, zero apps, zero funding. Risk: grief monetizes poorly, episodic use.

### 🥉 Gambling recovery
- **Loudest verified demand: ~180,000/mo "gambling addiction help"** (JAMA, cross-confirmed),
  ~20M affected, sports betting legal in 38 states. But the validated model (Birches, $20M) is
  clinical/insurance-reimbursed — wrong *shape* for a solo; indie slice = blocker+community
  (thin moat).

### Avoid (bloodbaths)
Porn/NoFap (commoditized), screen-time (Opal/one sec/Forest entrenched), general
menopause/meditation/grief (crowded; menopause's money is capital-heavy HRT-telehealth).
