# Argument Black Box — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A flight recorder for fights. Couples opt in; on-device, it listens during
conflicts and afterward shows *patterns*, not transcripts — the brutal mirror no therapist
is in the room to hold up.

**Category / niche:** Emotional intelligence / relationships.

## Insight (the wedge)
People don't lack emotional advice; they lack *self-awareness in the moment*. EI apps teach
concepts in the calm; nobody shows you your own tape from the storm. "You interrupt within
4 seconds when finances come up" changes behavior in a way a listicle never will.

## Target user
Couples who fight in circles and want to break the pattern, but won't (or can't) go to
therapy weekly. Also solo users who want to see their own conflict habits.

## Signature mechanic
**Pattern playback**: on-device processing during a (consented) conflict extracts *patterns*
— interruption latency, who escalates first, recurring trigger topics, tone shifts — and
presents them afterward as insight, never as a raw transcript to re-litigate.

## MVP scope
1. Explicit dual-consent "record this argument" mode (privacy-first, on-device).
2. On-device analysis → pattern summary (no cloud transcript).
3. Post-conflict insight card ("here's what happened structurally").
4. Trend view across arguments (are we improving?).
5. Gentle, non-blaming framing + optional shared review.

## Why it stands out
Nobody's productized in-the-moment conflict data for consumers. The mirror is the product;
the restraint (patterns, not transcripts) is the trust.

## Monetization
Couples subscription; higher WTP than solo wellness.

## Top risk → mitigation
Privacy + weaponization (using recordings as ammo) → strict on-device processing, no stored
transcript, patterns-only output, and dual consent enforced by design.

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design an "argument black box" app for couples: with dual consent, it listens on-device
> during a fight and afterward shows behavioral *patterns* (interruption latency, who
> escalates, recurring triggers, tone shifts) — never a raw transcript. Spec the 5 MVP screens
> with copy, the consent + privacy model (on-device, no stored transcript), the post-conflict
> insight card, and the trend view. Voice: neutral, non-blaming, almost clinical-calm — the
> product is a mirror, not a referee.
