# Behavioral-Addiction Whitespace — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A "boring is the goal" app for the behavioral addictions nobody treats —
porn, doomscrolling, gambling, vaping — the ones too embarrassing or too new for the
AA/chips/higher-power model. Anonymous, no shame, just friction + data.

**Category / niche:** Behavioral / process addiction.

## Insight (the wedge)
Alcohol and drugs have a century of treatment infrastructure. The *behavioral* addictions
exploding right now — porn, scrolling, gambling, vaping — have almost none. Massive demand,
thin supply. And the old recovery model (public meetings, chips, "higher power") doesn't fit a
young, private, secular, embarrassed user. Build the *modern* recovery primitive.

## Target user
People hooked on a behavior they're too embarrassed to discuss and that has no established
program: compulsive porn/scrolling/gambling/vaping users, mostly younger, privacy-obsessed.

## Signature mechanic
**Friction + data, no dogma**: real-time friction (blocking/interruption at the urge moment) +
the relapse-autopsy data loop + an anonymous, no-chips, no-higher-power framing. Recovery
stripped of the religiosity and public confession that repel this user.

## MVP scope
1. Pick your behavior (single-vertical to start; expandable).
2. Urge-moment friction/interruption.
3. Relapse autopsy → danger map (see `relapse-autopsy.md`).
4. Anonymous, identity-light progress (no real name ever).
5. A modern, secular "reps"/awareness metric (no streak resets).

## Why it stands out
Occupies the gap between "willpower app with no depth" and "clinical program that doesn't fit
me." Whoever builds the default modern primitive for behavioral addiction owns a category with
huge demand and almost no incumbents. (Verified: gambling-help search ~180k/mo; porn-help
~30.7k/mo — loud demand, thin serious tooling.)

## Monetization
Subscription; potential to expand into multiple verticals from one engine.

## Top risk → mitigation
Low moat / commoditized (esp. porn) → differentiate on the emotional/data depth and
cross-vertical engine, not on blocking alone; vulnerable-user duty of care (esp. gambling).

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design the modern "recovery primitive" for behavioral addictions with no established program
> — porn, doomscrolling, gambling, vaping. It rejects the AA model (no chips, no higher power,

> relapse-autopsy data loop and a no-reset awareness metric. Spec the 5 MVP screens with copy,
> the single-vertical MVP + expandable engine, the anonymity model, and the duty-of-care
> safeguards. Voice: matter-of-fact, non-preachy, "boring is the goal."
