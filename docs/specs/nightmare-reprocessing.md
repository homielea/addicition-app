# Nightmare Reprocessing — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** A lucid-dreaming app that ignores "fly over a city for fun" and instead
trains people with recurring nightmares to turn, inside the dream, and face the thing —
a painkiller, not a toy.

**Category / niche:** Sleep / dreams / mental wellness.

## Insight (the wedge)
"Controlling dreams for fun" is a party trick with a two-week shelf life — that's why lucid
apps churn. But nightmares and trauma dreams are a *painful, recurring* problem people pay to
fix indefinitely. There's a real clinical technique here — Image Rehearsal Therapy — that no
consumer product has properly built around.

## Target user
People with recurring nightmares or trauma-linked dreams wrecking their sleep. High intent,
high pain, poorly served by generic "dream journal" apps.

## Signature mechanic
**Rehearse-and-face**: awake, the user rewrites the nightmare's ending and rehearses it
(IRT-style) + trains lucidity cues; in-dream, a single pre-loaded intention ("turn and ask
it what it wants") gives them one brave move. The app logs whether they took it.

## MVP scope
1. Nightmare capture + pattern tracking (recurring themes).
2. Guided rewrite/rehearsal of a new ending (IRT-based).
3. Daytime reality-check / lucidity training tied to the nightmare's cues.
4. Morning debrief: did the dream shift? did you face it?
5. Progress view: nightmare frequency/intensity over time.

## Why it stands out
Turns a churny novelty toy into a retained painkiller with a clinical spine — and a
measurable outcome (nightmare frequency down) you can market honestly.

## Monetization
Subscription; potential clinical/therapist-adjacent partnerships (adjunct to PTSD care).

## Top risk → mitigation
Trauma/duty-of-care surface → position as a wellness/self-help adjunct, include crisis
resources, do not claim to treat PTSD; consider clinician review of the IRT flow.

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design a sleep app for people plagued by *recurring nightmares* (not lucid-dreaming
> hobbyists). It uses Image Rehearsal Therapy: awake, users rewrite and rehearse a new ending;
> it trains lucidity cues tied to the nightmare; in-dream they carry one pre-loaded intention
> to face the thing. Spec the 5 MVP screens with copy, the rewrite/rehearsal flow, the
> outcome metric (nightmare frequency/intensity), and a careful, non-clinical-but-serious
> tone with crisis safeguards. Make it feel like relief, not a game.
