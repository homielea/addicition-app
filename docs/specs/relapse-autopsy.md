# Relapse Autopsy — Concept Spec (for Fable 5)

**One-liner:** An addiction app that reframes relapse from failure into the single most
valuable data event — a 60-second structured debrief that builds a personal "danger map."

**Category / niche:** Addiction / behavior change (works across any habit).

## Insight (the wedge)
Shame causes churn — people delete the app the moment they slip. Treat the slip as the *most
valuable* data point instead of the shameful one and you keep the user exactly when they need
you most. (This mechanic is also baked into Trojan Horse; here it's the whole product.)

## Target user
Anyone in a quit/recovery process who spirals and disengages after a slip — across alcohol,
porn, gambling, vaping, doomscrolling.

## Signature mechanic
**The autopsy**: right after a slip, a fast, shame-free 3-step debrief — what set it off
(trigger), what you were actually feeling underneath, one optional note — that aggregates into
a **danger map** of your top triggers and risky times, which then powers prevention.

## MVP scope
1. Low-friction "I slipped — log it" entry (no shame).
2. 60-second 3-step autopsy (trigger / feeling / note).
3. Danger map: ranked triggers + risky time-of-day patterns.
4. Prevention nudges derived from the map.
5. A "reps/insight" progress metric that only goes up (slips count).

## Why it stands out
It's the anti-shame retention mechanic as a standalone product — the moment every other app
loses the user is the moment this one deepens the relationship.

## Monetization
Subscription; strong retention story makes LTV the pitch.

## Top risk → mitigation
Thin as a single feature → pair with prevention (the danger map must *do* something) so it's a
loop, not just journaling.

## Fable 5 build prompt
> Design an addiction app built entirely around the "relapse autopsy": after a slip, a
> 60-second shame-free debrief (trigger → underlying feeling → optional note) that aggregates
> into a personal "danger map" of top triggers and risky times, which then drives prevention
> nudges. Spec the 5 MVP screens with copy, the autopsy flow, the danger-map logic, and a
> progress metric that only increases (slips count as data). Voice: calm, non-judgmental,
> "your slip is intel, not failure."
